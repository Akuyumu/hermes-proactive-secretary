Applying for a scholarship at Example University

If you wish to be considered for a scholarship, submit the online scholarship application immediately after the admission application. The scholarship application deadline is the same as the admission deadline.

What to do and expect

  1. Apply online: select the scholarship types and list your top three achievements.
  2. After you submit: upload a passport-sized photo and send the referee link.
  3. Interview: shortlisted candidates are contacted by email.

Scholarships for current students are open from 1 to 31 July every year. Current students are notified by email when the application window opens.
